# Glossary conflicts awaiting sign-off

These are places where the finished books rendered a term differently from the master.
Nothing here has been applied, and each book keeps its own rendering in its own glossary.
To adopt a book's rendering, run `python -m scripts.glossary merge runs/<slug>/work/glossary.json --only <arabic>`.

Start with `أحمد`: the master holds it as "Ahmad (a student present)", a one-off from a lecture
that would mislead any book citing Imam Ahmad.
