# Glossary Seed Editorial Notes

This file records cross-book conflicts resolved by editorial choice during the creation of the master glossary seed.

## Resolved Conflicts

### 1. التمكين (tamkin)

**Master rendering:** "empowerment; being established in the land"

**Conflict sources:**
- Anwar used: "empowerment"
- Bawsala used: "establishment"

**Resolution:** Merged both senses into a single phrase to capture the full semantic range of the term. Can be reviewed and refined after sign-off using `scripts/glossary.py merge` if needed.

### 2. السنن الإلهية (al-sunan al-ilahiyyah)

**Master rendering:** "the divine norms — God's unchanging ways"

**Conflict sources:**
- Anwar used: "divine patterns"
- Bawsala used: "divine norms"

**Resolution:** Selected "norms" as the master term for its theological precision. Can be reviewed and refined after sign-off using `scripts/glossary.py merge` if needed.

---

These editorial decisions can be revisited and adjusted through the merge workflow once the glossary enters the harvest + sign-off phase.
