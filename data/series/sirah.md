# Series notes — سيرة رسول الله ﷺ (runs `sirah-NN`)

Read at Prime (SKILL.md step 2) and fold into `DISPATCH.md`; update at Finish with anything
the new episode established. These are conventions the glossary can't carry — renderings we

- **Speaker:** Ahmad al-Sayyid (أحمد السيد) — same author as the translate-islamic-book
  master glossary, so his terminology is already well covered there.
- **Series title:** the Prophetic biography series, سيرة رسول الله ﷺ — "The Biography of the
  Messenger of Allah ﷺ".
- **Format:** live class with students; students answer questions (render replies with the
  dash convention), and the speaker reads long passages from a printed textbook.
- **Textbook:** Sirat Ibn Hisham — the speaker reads from it constantly; ASR often garbles
  the title (e.g. 'سيره الشام'). Editions/commentaries that recur: al-Rawd al-Unuf
  (al-Suhayli), Zad al-Ma'ad (Ibn al-Qayyim).
- **Coined renderings (ours, keep unless a published English title surfaces):**
  - أبجديات الثقافة → "The ABCs of Culture (Abjadiyyat al-Thaqafah)" — the speaker's earlier
    series; no published English title known.
  - السيرة النبوية للمصلحين → "The Prophetic Biography for Reformers (al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah
    lil-Muslihin)" — the speaker's own earlier sirah series; ASR garbles it ('المستحين').
- **Numbers:** digits, not words.
